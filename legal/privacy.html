Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This service lets people host giveaways and lets other people enter them. This page explains what data it handles and where that data goes. Questions: {{CONTACT}}.

What we collect

Hosts

When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We ask for one Drive permission, drive.file. It lets the service see and edit only the files it creates for you: a "Giveaway Host" folder and one Google Sheet per giveaway. It cannot open any other file in your Drive.

We store an encrypted copy of your Google refresh token so the service can keep saving entries to your Sheet while you are away.

People who enter a giveaway

When you enter, you give a name, an email address, any other answers the host asked for, and which bonus ways to enter you ticked. The service writes this straight into the host's Google Sheet with a timestamp. We do not keep a separate copy. To block duplicate entries, the server holds the list of entered email addresses in memory. It is cleared whenever the server restarts.

Technical data

Your IP address is held in memory for a few minutes to limit repeated submissions. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Who can see it

The host of a giveaway sees the entries to it. The host decides how to use that information and is responsible for it. If you have a question about your entry, ask the host. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.

Cookies

Hosts get one signed session cookie that keeps them signed in for up to 14 days. People who enter a giveaway get no cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Services we rely on

  • Google, for sign-in, Drive and Sheets.
  • A web hosting provider, which runs the server.
  • A database provider, which stores host account records, the encrypted Google refresh tokens and a map from giveaway ID to host. It does not store entries.

Google user data

This service's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to run the features you see in the app, and is never used for advertising or shared with others.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep a host's account record until the host asks us to delete it. Hosts can cut off access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting a giveaway in the dashboard moves its Sheet to your Drive trash. Entries live in the host's Sheet, so hosts can edit or delete them there, and people who entered can ask the host to remove theirs.

Children

This service is not aimed at children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country if it is higher.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
